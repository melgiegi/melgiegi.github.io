Privacy Policy for Legion Clash

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Developer: Sudoja Games · Version 0.12.0

This policy explains how Legion Clash handles information. The game has no player account or login. Campaign progress is saved on your device. The campaign can be played offline; advertising, purchases, and analytics use network services when available.

Gameplay analytics

We use Unity Analytics to understand usage, diagnose problems, and improve the game. Analytics can include gameplay milestones, campaign stage starts and results, attempts, session timing, formation-change indicators, skill-use counts, app version, advertising interactions and impression revenue. Unity's SDK also processes usage and diagnostic information, device/app identifiers and technical information about the device, operating system and application. These identifiers can distinguish installations or devices; this information is not described as anonymous.

Analytics collection starts when the service initializes in a player build. There is currently no in-game analytics opt-out. The app may queue events while offline and send them when a connection is available. We do not send the contents of your local save as a cloud backup.

Advertising and purchases

The app uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads and ironSource advertising services. Advertising providers may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions and performance, and device/diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. We do not request precise GPS location. Optional purchases are handled through Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing. Purchase and entitlement information is used to grant and restore purchases; we do not receive your payment card details.

Provider handling is described in Unity's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy. The app's use of these services means information may leave your device.

Local progress and notifications

Army, currencies, progression and settings are stored locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local progress, subject to any device backup behavior. This does not itself request deletion of information already sent to service providers.

Optional reward reminders use Unity Mobile Notifications and Android's local notification system. With permission, the app schedules reminders on your device for a full idle chest and a daily reward. Android may request POST_NOTIFICATIONS. These reminders do not use a server push service or upload a push token. You can disable them in game settings or Android notification settings.

Permissions and choices

Network and network-state permissions support online services. Advertising SDKs declare com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID. Notification permission is optional and is not needed to play. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permission. Advertising identifier controls are available in Android settings.

Retention and requests

Local progress remains until removed on the device. Information processed by Unity and Google is subject to their applicable retention practices; clearing local progress does not guarantee deletion from their systems. Contact us about access or deletion requests so we can help identify the applicable service and process. The app does not currently include an in-game analytics deletion control.

Children's privacy

Legion Clash is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can help address the concern.
