Privacy Policy for Legion Clash

Last updated: 17 August 2026 · Developer: Sudoja Games

This Privacy Policy explains how Legion Clash ("the App", "we", "us"), published by Sudoja Games, handles information when you use it. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy.

The short version

Legion Clash does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no account system, no login, and does not require an internet connection to play. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or files.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress — such as your level, currencies, unlocked troops, formation, and settings — is saved locally in the App's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this progress is permanently deleted.

Children's privacy

Legion Clash is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no data at all, no personal information from children is gathered, used, or disclosed.

Permissions

The App requests only the standard permissions required to run and, where present, the Android system permission needed to store your game save. It does not request access to sensitive device features.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App changes — for example, if a future version introduces advertising or online features. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where required, update the App's Data Safety information on Google Play. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
