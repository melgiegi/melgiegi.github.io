Privacy Policy for Legion Clash

Last updated: 20 August 2026 · Developer: Sudoja Games

This Privacy Policy explains how Legion Clash ("the App", "we", "us"), published by Sudoja Games, handles information when you use it. By installing or using the App you agree to this policy.

The short version

Legion Clash does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The App has no account system and no login, and your game progress is saved only on your own device. The App does show advertisements, and the advertising providers listed below use a device advertising identifier to serve them — that is the only information connected with the App that leaves your device. An internet connection is needed to load ads; the game itself is playable offline.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or files.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not sell your data, and we never receive personal information about you from our advertising providers — we only see aggregate, anonymous reports of how many ads were shown.

Advertising

Legion Clash shows advertisements to keep the game free. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies), which mediates ads from Unity Ads and the ironSource Exchange. Watching an ad is optional for bonus rewards; a short ad may also appear between battles.

To serve and measure ads, these providers may collect and process a device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), along with technical information such as your device model, operating system version, coarse country, and how you interacted with the ad. This information is handled by those providers under their own privacy policies, not by us:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Your choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by device). Deleting the ID stops personalised advertising; you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress — such as your level, currencies, unlocked troops, formation, and settings — is saved locally in the App's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this progress is permanently deleted.

Children's privacy

Legion Clash is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the App serves advertising that relies on a device advertising identifier, it is intended for players aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App in a way that concerns you, contact us at the address below and we will assist where we are able.

Permissions

The App requests internet and network-state access (needed to load ads) and the Android advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), which the advertising SDK declares. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App changes — for example, if a future version adds a new advertising provider or online feature. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where required, update the App's Data Safety information on Google Play. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
