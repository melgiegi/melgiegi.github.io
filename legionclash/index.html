Win the fight before it starts

You never control units mid-battle. You recruit them, merge them into stronger tiers, and place them on the grid — then watch your plan hold or fall apart.

What's in the game

⚔️ Arrange your formation

Drop troop cards onto a 5×4 grid. Melee up front, ranged behind. Every unit costs Capacity, so smarter placement fields a bigger army.

🛡️ Auto-battle, your way

No twitch reflexes, no frantic tapping. Speed controls let you savour a comeback or blitz an easy win at 3×.

🔺 Merge & upgrade

Recruit duplicates and merge them into higher tiers. A pile of common soldiers becomes the elite squad that carries your whole line.

👑 Conquer the campaign

Escalating stages with new enemy types and a boss every five levels — Minotaurs, Trolls, and worse.

🎯 Collect 23 troops

Knights, archers, mages and captured monsters across Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary tiers. Every one of them is recruitable.

💰 Idle gold & daily rewards

Your legion keeps earning while you're away, and login streaks grow. Come back to a fatter treasury and push further.
