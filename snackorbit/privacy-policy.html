Privacy Policy

Snack Orbit · Developed by Sudoja Games
Effective date: 20 September 2026 · Last updated: 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoja Games ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you play Snack Orbit (the "Game", package name com.sudoja.snackorbit). By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location. The Game and the third-party advertising services it uses may automatically collect the following:

  • Device & identifiers: device model, operating system version, language, and a mobile advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked, and how long a session lasted.
  • Approximate location: a coarse, country or region level location that may be inferred from your IP address. We never collect precise GPS location.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data used to fix bugs and improve stability.

2. Your Game Progress Stays On Your Device

Your coins, upgrades, menu items, missions, and customer album are saved locally on your device only, together with a backup copy. This progress is never uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the Game, that progress is deleted with it.

The Game includes an export and import feature that copies your save data to and from your device clipboard. This is performed by you, on your device, and the data is not transmitted to us.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • To display advertisements, including personalized ads where permitted.
  • To measure advertising performance.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

The Game is free and supported by advertising. We use the following third-party services, which may collect and process data (including your advertising identifier) under their own privacy policies:

    Privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only with:

6. Your Choices & Controls

  • Personalized ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device settings:
    Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstall: You may stop all further data collection by uninstalling the Game. This also deletes your locally stored progress.

7. Data Retention

We do not operate servers that store your data. Advertising and diagnostic data are retained by our third-party providers according to their own retention policies.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
